What data Bounce Royale uses, why, who handles it and how you can exercise your rights.

In short

  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress (record, skins, cards, boxes, coins and settings) is stored only on your phone. We have no servers with player data.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first, and you can change it in Settings › "Privacy options".
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

1. Who is responsible

Bounce Royale is an independent game developed by Luis Angel Caballero Jardón, based in Metepec, State of Mexico, Mexico, who is responsible for processing the data described in this policy. For any privacy matter, write to soporte.bounceroyale@outlook.com.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't have our own server and we don't receive your matches.
  • We don't build profiles about you or share your progress with third parties.
  • There's no chat or contact with other players: in VS Mode you compete against rivals controlled by the game.

4. Ads and consent

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner only during a match, an ad when the app opens and rewarded videos that you choose to watch to earn boxes. Google may use cookies or device identifiers to show ads and measure their performance. You can read how in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent (European Union, United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions)

When you open the game, if the law in your region requires it, you'll see Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform, compatible with the IAB TCF framework). You can accept, reject or choose which purposes you allow. If you don't accept, ads won't be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › "Privacy options".

United States

If you live in a state with a privacy law (for example, California), you can ask that your data not be sold or shared for advertising through the Google privacy options shown in the app.

iPhone: App Tracking Transparency

Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) can only be used if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you don't allow it, or if the prompt isn't shown to you, the app doesn't access the IDFA.

How to limit personalized ads

  • In the app: Settings › "Privacy options".
  • Android: Settings › Google › Ads › Delete advertising ID.
  • iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.
  • You can also remove ads with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase.

5. In-app purchases

Box packs and "Remove ads" are paid through the App Store or Google Play, which process the payment under their own privacy policies. To process the payment, the store uses the email of your Apple or Google account; the app doesn't store it. We only receive confirmation that the purchase was valid so we can deliver it. We never receive your card number or bank details.

6. Leaderboard

If you use the leaderboard, your score is sent to Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android) with the alias of your account on that service. On Android you're only asked to sign in when you tap the leaderboard button. These services have their own privacy policies: Apple and Google.

7. Children

Bounce Royale is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child sent us personal information, write to us and we'll delete it. We recommend turning on the parental controls of your device and store for purchases.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Game progress: stays on your phone until you uninstall the app or clear its data; that deletes it completely.
  • Ad, purchase and leaderboard data: kept by Google and Apple under their own policies. You can manage it from your Google or Apple account.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to reply.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use (ARCO rights under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties), and you can withdraw your consent. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom you also have your GDPR rights (including portability and the right to complain to your data protection authority), and in California your CCPA/CPRA rights.

Since we don't keep player data on our own servers, the fastest way is almost always to clear the app's data or use Google's and Apple's privacy tools. If you need anything else, write to soporte.bounceroyale@outlook.com with the subject "Privacy". We reply within 20 business days at most.

10. Security and transfers

The app's communication with Apple, Google and AdMob is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). These providers may process data on servers outside Mexico, mainly in the United States, with the safeguards the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, if the change is significant, we'll let you know in the app or on the store listing.
