Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Write to soporte.bounceroyale@outlook.com. Tell us your phone model, system version and what you were doing. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap the screen to fly. The ball bounces from wall to wall and every wall bounce is a point. Slip through the gaps between the obstacles: hitting one ends the run. Coins appear in the gaps, so go for them if you dare.

How does Fever Mode work?

Chain 10 bounces in a row and the ball enters Fever Mode: you're invincible and you smash the next obstacle.

Who are my rivals in VS Mode?

Five rivals controlled by the game (AI). You all start in the same arena and the last ball standing wins. The top three make the podium.

How do I unlock skins?

There are 26. You start with the classic one, your best score unlocks 9 more (one every 50 points, from 50 to 450), and the other 16 come from mystery boxes. Each box gives 5 cards; collect enough cards of a skin and it's yours. The odds are in the shop under "View odds" and in the Terms of Use.

How do I get free boxes?

Claim the free daily box in the shop, and get up to 5 more boxes a day by watching a rewarded video. Sometimes there's no video available; it depends on your region and connection.

I bought "Remove ads". How do I get it on another device?

In the shop or in Settings, tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

I changed phones and lost my progress.

Your record, coins, boxes, cards and skins are stored only on your phone, so they don't move to a new one and we can't recover them. "Remove ads" can always be restored with "Restore purchases".

How does the leaderboard work?

Your best score goes to Game Center on iPhone and Google Play Games on Android. On Android you're only asked to sign in when you tap the leaderboard button.

How do I share my record?

Tap your record on the home screen. You'll get a card with your crowned skin and your best score that you can share as an image in any app.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In Settings › "Privacy options". You can also remove ads with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase. More in the Privacy Policy.

Which languages does it come in?

16. The game uses your phone's language, and if yours isn't one of them, it opens in English.
