About the game

Bounce Royale is a one-tap arcade game: tap to fly and the ball bounces from wall to wall, dodging obstacles, with coins waiting in the gaps. Every wall bounce is a point.

Chain 10 bounces in a row to trigger Fever Mode: you're invincible and you smash the next obstacle. In Adventure Mode the world changes as your score climbs, across 6 worlds from Clear Sky to The Glitch, all laid out on the Trophy Road. In VS Mode you face 5 AI rivals in the same arena; the last ball standing wins and the top three make the podium.

There are 26 skins: the classic one, 9 unlocked by your record and 16 from mystery boxes in 5 rarities (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Champion), with the odds shown in the game. There's a free box every day, a record card to share, and leaderboards on Game Center and Google Play Games.

Key points

  • One tap: learn it in seconds, short runs.
  • Fever Mode after 10 bounces in a row.
  • Adventure Mode with 6 worlds and VS Mode against 5 AI rivals.
  • 26 skins: earned with your record or from boxes with visible odds.
  • No sign-up, 16 languages.
  • Made with Flutter and Flame by an independent Mexican developer.
