4. VS Mode

In VS Mode you compete in the same arena against 5 rivals controlled by the game. It is not an online multiplayer mode against other people: there's no chat and no data exchange with other players.

Bounce Royale is free and all purchases are optional. In the app you can buy with real money:

6. Mystery boxes and odds

You can get free boxes (1 daily box and up to 5 more boxes a day by watching a rewarded video) or by buying packs. Each box contains 5 cards. Each card is picked at random from the 16 box skins, all with the same probability (6.25% each). That's why the chance of each rarity depends on how many skins it has:

Once you collect the cards a skin needs, you unlock it forever on that device. These odds are also shown in the app, in the shop, under "View odds".

Besides the 16 box skins there's a classic skin and 9 level skins that unlock for free with your best score, one every 50 points (from 50 to 450). No skin gives an advantage in the game.
