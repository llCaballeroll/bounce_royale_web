How to play

One finger.
Zero rest.

You learn it in seconds. Staying alive is the hard part.

  1. Tap to fly Every tap lifts the ball. Find the rhythm and stay in the air.
  2. Bounce wall to wall The ball crosses the screen from one wall to the other. Every wall bounce is a point.
  3. Dodge the obstacles Slip through the gaps between the spiked pipes. One hit and the run is over.
  4. Grab the coins Coins wait in the gaps. Go for them if you dare.

Fever Mode

10 bounces in a row.

VS Mode

You vs 5 AI rivals.
Last ball standing.

Six balls share one arena: yours and five rivals driven by the game. Bounce, dodge and hold on longer than everyone else.

  1. Six balls, one arena.You and 5 AI rivals start together.
  2. Survive.Every rival that crashes is one fewer between you and the crown.
  3. Take the podium.The top three make the podium, and the winner gets the crown.
